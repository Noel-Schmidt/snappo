Snappo v2.0.0 is here. We have reworked the experience from the ground up to make everyday developer tasks feel clearer, easier to find, and more pleasant to use.

This release brings a fresh design across Snappo, a more considered way to move between tools, and new places to find answers and follow what we are building.

## A fresh design across Snappo

The interface has been redesigned throughout the site. The homepage, tool catalog, navigation, and supporting pages now share a more consistent visual language, with clearer page structure and a calmer layout that keeps the task in focus.

The updated design also works across screen sizes and light and dark themes, so the experience stays familiar whether you are on a phone or at your desk.

## A smoother way to find and use tools

Snappo brings a growing collection of developer utilities together in one place. The refreshed navigation and tool catalog make it easier to browse or search for the tool you need. Tool pages put their inputs and results first, with explanations close by when a setting or output needs context.

Whether you are formatting JSON, generating a password, checking a webhook HMAC, or converting a timestamp, the goal is the same: get to the useful part quickly and understand what the tool is doing.

Explore the [developer tools](/tools) to see what is available.

## Answers in one place

We have added a dedicated [Snappo FAQ](/faq) with detailed answers about the tools, privacy, and common questions. It gives new visitors a clearer place to start and makes it easier to understand how a tool handles its input before using it.

## Introducing the Snappo Blog

This blog is new in v2.0.0. It will bring together practical developer guides and news about Snappo. The first articles cover cron expressions, JSON syntax, URL encoding, password security, and HMAC verification.

Browse the [latest articles](/blog), and check back for more guides and product updates.

## Thank you for being here

Snappo v2.0.0 is a new foundation for the product and for the work ahead. We hope the refreshed experience helps you get through everyday development tasks with less friction. If you have an idea or spot something we can improve, send us feedback on [GitHub](https://github.com/noel-schmidt/snappo/issues).
